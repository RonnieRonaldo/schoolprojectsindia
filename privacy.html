Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how School Projects India handles information when you visit our website or purchase a digital project.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 loads only after you select “Accept analytics”. It helps us understand visits and interactions with our project pages. Google may process cookie identifiers, pages visited, device and browser information, approximate location and interactions. Analytics cookies can recognise your browser across visits. Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation consent remain off.

You can reject analytics and still browse, buy, download purchased files and use the contact form. Change your choice using “Analytics settings” at the bottom of any page. Withdrawing consent disables further collection through our Analytics tag and removes accessible first-party Analytics cookies. It does not erase information already collected by Google. Closing the banner does not grant consent or change an existing choice. Read how Google uses information from sites using its services.

Purchases and Payments

Purchases are processed by Paddle.com, which acts as Merchant of Record. Payment details are handled by Paddle and its payment partners rather than directly by School Projects India. Paddle processes personal information according to its own privacy notice and legal obligations.

Digital Fulfilment

Our systems may process technical transaction identifiers needed to verify that a purchase was completed and to provide secure access to the correct digital PDF. We do not use these identifiers as payment-card information.

Cookies and Local Storage

Your analytics choice is saved in local storage for up to 180 days. If storage is blocked, the choice may not persist between pages. Separately, product pages use browser storage for transaction references that help restore access to purchased downloads. Rejecting analytics does not remove those references. Clearing all this site’s browser data removes your saved analytics choice and download references.

Hosting and digital delivery

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website and provides our download infrastructure through Workers and R2. Technical request information, including IP addresses and browser information, may be processed to deliver and protect the site and verify downloads. These services are separate from optional analytics. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Contact form

Your name, email address and message are sent to Web3Forms to deliver your enquiry. We use them to reply and provide help. Web3Forms also processes technical request data to operate its service and prevent spam. See Web3Forms’ privacy policy. Please do not include passwords or payment card details. Sending a message does not require accepting analytics.

YouTube and payments

Embedded YouTube players may send technical information to YouTube when they load or when you play a video. Rejecting our Google Analytics does not block these separate players. See Google’s privacy policy. Paddle checkout and purchased downloads also work independently of analytics consent. See Paddle’s privacy policy.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights of access, correction, deletion or objection. Mandatory privacy rights under applicable law are not limited by this policy.

Contact

For privacy questions relating specifically to School Projects India, please use the contact details provided on our website. For personal information processed by Paddle, please use Paddle's privacy and buyer-support channels.
