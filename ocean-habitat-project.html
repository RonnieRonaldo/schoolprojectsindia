Ocean Habitat Project for Kids

Need an ocean habitat project for school? This printable 3D model helps children create a colourful underwater scene with sea animals, coral and layered ocean scenery for EVS homework, habitat lessons and school assignments.

An Easy Ocean Project for Busy Parents

If your child suddenly needs an ocean habitat model, sea animals project or EVS assignment for school, this printable project gives you an easy starting point.

The backgrounds, sea animals and underwater scenery are already illustrated. Children still do the creative work themselves by printing, cutting, arranging and building their own 3D ocean habitat.

What Children Can Learn

Building an ocean habitat model gives children a visual way to explore life under the sea. They can learn about marine animals, coral reefs, underwater plants and the different parts of an ocean ecosystem.

The finished model can be used for EVS projects, animal habitat lessons, sea creature activities, environmental studies, homework and school exhibitions.
