Rainforest Habitat Project for Kids

Create an easy and colourful 3D rainforest habitat project for school using a printable paper model. This project is ideal for EVS activities, animal habitat lessons, rainforest assignments and creative homework.

Easy 3D Rainforest Model for School

A rainforest habitat project is a fun way for children to learn about tropical animals, plants and the different layers of the rainforest while creating a colourful 3D model.

This printable project includes rainforest scenery, tropical plants and animals that can be cut out and arranged in layers. It can be used for EVS projects, habitat lessons, ecosystem studies and environmental school assignments.

How to Make the Rainforest Habitat Project

1. Print

Print the project pages on A4 paper or slightly thicker paper.

2. Cut

Carefully cut out the rainforest animals, plants and scenery pieces.

3. Create

Glue the pieces into a box or on cardboard to create your own 3D rainforest model.

Perfect for EVS and Rainforest Projects

This rainforest model can be useful for students working on topics such as rainforest habitats, tropical animals, rainforest layers, ecosystems, food chains and environmental studies.

Parents can print the project pages at home, while children can do the cutting, arranging and decorating themselves.

Get the Printable Rainforest Project

What You Need

You only need a printer, A4 paper, scissors, glue and a box or cardboard base. You can also add leaves, green paper, sticks or other craft materials to make the rainforest scene even more detailed.
