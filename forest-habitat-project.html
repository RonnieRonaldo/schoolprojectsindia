Forest Habitat Project for Kids

Need a forest project for school? This printable 3D forest habitat gives children an easy starting point for an EVS assignment, habitat project or ecosystem model. Print the pages at home, cut out the pieces and build the model together.

A Simple Starting Point for Parents

School projects can sometimes arrive with only a topic and a deadline. If your child needs to make a forest habitat model, this project gives you the main scenery and animals without removing the creative part of the assignment.

You can help with printing and preparation, while your child cuts, arranges, glues and decorates the finished model. Extra leaves, sticks, stones or craft materials can still be added to make every project different.

What Children Can Learn

A forest habitat project can help children explore how plants and animals live together in an ecosystem. It can also be used as a starting point for discussions about animal habitats, food chains, trees, woodland animals and environmental studies.

This makes the project useful for EVS homework, class activities, habitat assignments, exhibitions and creative school work.
