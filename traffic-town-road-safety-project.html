A Complete Paper Town to Build and Explore

Arrange the buildings and standing pieces around the crossroads. Use the model to talk about pedestrian crossings, road signs and travelling to school. This is a printable paper model, not an electronic working traffic signal.

You need: an A4 printer, paper, scissors, glue and cardboard for a firm base. Younger children need adult help with cutting.

Print the Templates and Build the Road Base

Follow the included guide, cut out the four road sections and arrange them on cardboard. Match the road edges before gluing them down.

Add the School, Bookshop and Park

Cut and fold the standing pieces, then arrange your town. The school, bookshop and park give children familiar destinations for their road safety stories.

Traffic Light Models, Road Signs and People

Add the traffic signal models, road signs and vehicles. The paper schoolchildren, adults and auto-rickshaw help create a setting that feels familiar to families in India.

Road Safety Activities for School or Home

The four included cards are Walk to School, Bus Stop, Spot the Problem and Design Your Town. Let children move the figures, describe a route and explain their choices. Parents and teachers can use the scene for discussion alongside their own road safety guidance.

This road safety project can support an EVS activity, a classroom display or creative homework. Check the teacher’s requirements before choosing it for an assignment.
