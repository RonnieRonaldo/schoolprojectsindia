Help Children Explain Their Forest Model

For an EVS presentation, encourage your child to point to the model while explaining how living things use their habitat. Practise these short sentences, then let them use their own words:

  • “A habitat is a place where a living thing finds what it needs to survive.”
  • “My model shows a forest with trees, plants and animals.”
  • “Trees and other plants can provide food and shelter for forest animals.”

A Simple Forest Habitat Activity

Choose one animal shown in the model. Find out what it eats and where it shelters, then add a small handwritten label with those facts. Discuss how trees and plants help that animal.

For an Indian geography connection, choose a forest in India and compare its animals and plants with your woodland scene. This kit is a general forest model, not a replica of a specific Indian forest. Only identify a species as local after checking a suitable reference.
