Ocean Habitat Project for Kids

Create an easy and colourful 3D ocean habitat project for school using a printable paper model. This project is ideal for EVS activities, sea animal projects, ecosystem assignments and creative homework.

Easy 3D Ocean Model for School

An ocean habitat project is a fun way for children to learn about sea animals, underwater plants and marine ecosystems while building a colourful 3D model.

This printable project includes ocean scenery, coral, underwater plants and sea animals that can be cut out and arranged in layers. It is suitable for EVS projects, marine life assignments, habitat lessons and environmental studies.

How to Make the Ocean Habitat Project

1. Print

Print the project pages on A4 paper or slightly thicker paper.

2. Cut

Cut out the sea animals, coral, plants and underwater scenery pieces.

3. Create

Glue the pieces into a box or on cardboard to create your own 3D ocean model.

Perfect for EVS and Sea Animal Projects

This ocean model can be useful for students working on topics such as ocean habitats, sea animals, marine ecosystems, food chains, environmental studies and water life.

Parents can print the project pages at home, while children can enjoy cutting, arranging and decorating the model themselves.

Get the Printable Ocean Project

What You Need

You only need a printer, A4 paper, scissors, glue and a box or cardboard base. You can also add blue paper, sand, shells or craft materials to make the underwater scene even more detailed.
