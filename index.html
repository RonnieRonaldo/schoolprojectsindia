Made for Busy Parents Too

School project work can arrive at the worst possible moment. Instead of searching for hours for ideas and materials, these projects give you a clear starting point that you can prepare at home.

The goal is not to do the project for your child. You prepare the materials, and your child can cut, arrange, build, decorate and learn while creating the final model.
