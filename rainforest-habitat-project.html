Rainforest Habitat Project for Kids

Need a rainforest habitat project for school? This printable 3D model helps children build a colourful tropical rainforest with jungle animals, plants and layered scenery for EVS homework, ecosystem lessons and school assignments.

A Helpful Rainforest Project for Busy Parents

If your child needs a rainforest model, jungle animals project or EVS assignment, this printable project gives you a clear starting point. The main backgrounds, plants and animals are already illustrated, so children can focus on cutting, arranging and building the model.

They can choose where each animal should live inside the rainforest scene and add their own craft materials, leaves or decorations to make the project more personal.

What Children Can Learn

A rainforest habitat model helps children explore tropical ecosystems in a visual way. They can learn about rainforest animals, dense vegetation, different habitat layers and the relationship between plants and animals.

The project can be used for EVS activities, rainforest ecosystem lessons, animal habitat projects, environmental studies, homework and school exhibitions.

Meet Some of the Rainforest Animals

The printable set contains a variety of tropical animals and plants. Children can cut out animals such as a sloth, toucan, orangutan, anteater and other rainforest species and place them throughout the 3D habitat.

Example printable animal page from the rainforest project.

Clear Visual Building Instructions

The printable pages use simple visual symbols to show how the backgrounds and 3D pieces fit together. This makes it easier for children and parents to understand where pieces should be cut, folded and attached.

How to Make the Rainforest Habitat Project

1

Print

Print the rainforest project pages at home on A4 paper or slightly thicker paper.

2

Cut out the jungle animals, plants, backgrounds and other rainforest scenery.

3

Build

Arrange and glue the pieces inside a shoebox or similar cardboard box to create the layered 3D habitat.
